PulangBato Falls

Discover the Natural Beauty of Valencia, Negros Oriental.

Location: Barangay Malabo, Valencia, Negros Oriental.

Welcome to Pulangbato Falls, one of the beautiful natural attractions in Valencia, Negros Oriental. Discover the beauty of nature, enjoy the peaceful surroundings, and experience a refreshing escape from the busy city.

About the Destination

Pulangbato Falls is a popular tourist destination in Negros Oriental and it is known for its beautiful waterfall and distinctive reddish-orange rock formations. Its name comes from the Cebuano words "pula," meaning red, and "bato," meaning rock. The natural scenery makes it a wonderful place for visitors who appreciate nature and meaningful outdoor experiences.

Fun Activities

Here are some enjoyable activities at Pulangbato Falls:

  • Enjoying the beautiful scenery of the waterfall.
  • Taking pictures of the natural surroundings.
  • Relaxing and spending quality time with family and friends.
  • Exploring and appreciating the surrounding nature.
  • Enjoying the refreshing atmosphere of the destination.

How to Get There

Starting point: Valencia Town Center
Suggested Directions:

  1. Start your journey at Valencia Town Center.
  2. Travel toward Barangay Malabo.
  3. Follow the local road leading to Pulangbato Falls.
  4. Look for the entrance and follow the resort's directions.
  5. Arrive at Pulangbato Falls and enjoy your visit.

Please note that these directions are general directions
Please contact your local tour guide for the specific direction

Visitor Information

Entrance Fee:

  • For Adults: ₱100 per Person
  • Children: ₱50 for children aged 10 and below

Optional Fees

  • Locker - ₱100
  • Table - ₱50
  • Cottage - ₱300 to ₱600
  • Red Rock Hot Spring - ₱100 per Adult

Opening Hours: 8:00 AM – 6:00 PM daily.

Please Note that Entrance Fee, Optional Fee, and Opening Hours may change
